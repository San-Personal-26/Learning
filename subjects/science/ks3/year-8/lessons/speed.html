Speed

What speed actually is

Speed tells you how much distance something covers in a given amount of time. A "fast" thing covers more distance for the same time than a "slow" one does.

How it's measured

Speed, distance and time are connected by one relationship: speed = distance ÷ time. Once you know any two of them, you can always find the third — the triangle on the right is a quick way to remember which way round to divide or multiply.

A bit of history: why ships measure speed in "knots"

Before speedometers existed, sailors threw a wooden board tied to a rope full of evenly-spaced knots off the back of a moving ship, and counted how many knots ran out in 30 seconds. More knots in the same time meant a faster ship — that's still the origin of "knots" as a unit of speed today.

Why it matters

Speed cameras, Usain Bolt's 100 m sprint time, an aeroplane's cruising speed, even a weather forecast's wind speed — anywhere something moves, someone is measuring or calculating its speed.

O.1.1

Finding speed

If you know how far something travelled and how long it took, divide distance by time.

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. Speed = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h.

Watch your units

Speed can be measured in different units — km/h, mph (miles per hour), or m/s. Scientists usually use m/s. Always check which one a question wants.

O.1.2

Finding distance or time

The same relationship rearranges both ways: distance = speed × time, and time = distance ÷ speed.

A plane flies at 800 km/h for 3 hours → distance = 800 × 3 = 2400 km.
A train covers 300 km at 100 km/h → time = 300 ÷ 100 = 3 hours.

Common mix-up

"Average speed" isn't the same as what a speedometer shows at any one instant — a journey with stops and fast bits can still average out to one steady number.

Recap

  • ✓ Speed tells you how far something travels in a given time: speed = distance ÷ time.
  • ✓ Rearrange the same relationship to find distance (speed × time) or time (distance ÷ speed) instead.
  • ✓ Always check your units — km/h, m/s and mph aren't interchangeable without converting.
  • ✓ Average speed can hide a journey that sped up and slowed down along the way.
