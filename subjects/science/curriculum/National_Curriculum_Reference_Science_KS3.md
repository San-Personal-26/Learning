# National Curriculum Reference — Key Stage 3 Science

Source: DfE "National curriculum in England: science programmes of study" — Key Stage 3 subject content (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study).

Structured the same way the maths reference (`CURRICULUM_REFERENCE.md`) was — lettered main sections, numbered subsections — so each can be used as a short code, e.g. `B.13`, for lesson content to cite per CLAUDE.md's "cite the National Curriculum objective" rule.

**Important caveat:** exactly as with KS3 maths, the National Curriculum publishes Key Stage 3 (Years 7–9) science as a single combined programme of study — it does not split statements out by individual year group. The tables below are the complete KS3 content, grouped by the document's own named sub-headings (e.g. "Cells and organisation", "The periodic table") rather than by year. Deciding which refs map to Year 7 vs. Year 8 vs. Year 9 is a sequencing decision for this project to make, not something the source document specifies.

**Structure note:** the source organises KS3 science as Working scientifically, then three subject blocks — Biology, Chemistry, Physics — each broken into named topics. Each named topic below gets its own lettered section (so, unlike the maths reference's 6 broad strands, this one runs to 19 sections, A–S) because that's how granular the actual subject content is; strands aren't merged across topics the way maths' were.

**Sub-skill breakdowns** under each table are draft v1 — a further, lesson-sized decomposition of each broad NC statement, written independently for this project (not sourced from any third-party scheme). They're a starting point to refine as actual lesson content gets built, not a final spec. Refs follow the pattern `B.13.2` (strand B, NC statement 13, sub-skill 2).
