# National Curriculum Reference — Key Stage 4 Science

Source: DfE "National curriculum in England: science programmes of study" — Key Stage 4 subject content (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study).

Structured the same way the maths reference (`CURRICULUM_REFERENCE.md`) was — lettered main sections, numbered subsections — so each can be used as a short code, e.g. `K.8`, for lesson content to cite per CLAUDE.md's "cite the National Curriculum objective" rule.

**Important caveat:** as with KS3, the National Curriculum publishes Key Stage 4 (Years 10–11) science as a single combined programme of study, not split by individual year group. The source states that "curricula at key stage 4 should comprise approximately equal proportions of biology, chemistry and physics" — this is the statutory baseline that GCSE exam board specifications (Combined Science and the three separate sciences) are built from; it is not itself a specific exam board syllabus, and this project should go back to this text rather than a particular exam board's spec if a topic's framing is ambiguous, per CLAUDE.md.

**Non-core content:** a handful of statements are marked `[non-core]` below. In the source document these are content areas the DfE flags as required for GCSE separate sciences (triple award) but not for GCSE Combined Science (double award) — mostly the generator/motor effect applications and the whole of Space Physics. They're kept in for completeness rather than dropped, exactly as the maths reference kept the full KS3 table rather than pre-filtering it for Year 8.

**Structure note:** the source organises KS4 science as Working scientifically, then Biology, Chemistry and Physics, each broken into named topics — 27 in total, so this reference runs to sections A–AA (using a double letter past Z, spreadsheet-column style) rather than the 6 broad strands maths used.

**Sub-skill breakdowns** under each table are draft v1 — a further, lesson-sized decomposition of each broad NC statement, written independently for this project (not sourced from any third-party scheme, and not lifted from any exam board's specification). They're a starting point to refine as actual lesson content gets built, not a final spec. Refs follow the pattern `K.8.2` (strand K, NC statement 8, sub-skill 2).
